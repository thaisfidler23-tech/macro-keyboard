## Risks & Mitigation
- **False Positive Risk**: Normal performance variation triggers false alarms
  - Mitigation: Statistical significance testing and confidence intervals
- **Environment Variation Risk**: Test environment differences affect results
  - Mitigation: Controlled test environments and environment-specific baselines
- **Baseline Drift Risk**: Gradual degradation becomes new normal
  - Mitigation: Long-term trend analysis and manual baseline reviews
- **Pipeline Performance Risk**: Regression tests slow down development
  - Mitigation: Parallel test execution and optimized benchmark selection
