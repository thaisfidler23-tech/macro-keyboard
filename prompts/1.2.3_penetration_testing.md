## Risks & Mitigation
- **False Positive Risk**: Automated tools may report non-issues
  - Mitigation: Manual validation of all automated findings
- **System Disruption Risk**: Testing may impact system stability
  - Mitigation: Isolated testing environment with system backups
- **Scope Creep Risk**: Testing may reveal extensive issues requiring major changes
  - Mitigation: Prioritized remediation plan focusing on critical issues
- **Time Risk**: Thorough testing may take longer than estimated
  - Mitigation: Phased approach with continuous findings reporting
