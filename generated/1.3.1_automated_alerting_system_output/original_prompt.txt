## Risks & Mitigation
- **Alert Fatigue Risk**: Too many alerts may lead to users ignoring them
  - Mitigation: Smart throttling, alert aggregation, and tunable thresholds
- **Performance Impact Risk**: Alert processing may affect system performance
  - Mitigation: Asynchronous processing and efficient rule evaluation
- **False Positive Risk**: Incorrect alerts may reduce user trust
  - Mitigation: Intelligent baseline learning and alert validation
- **Notification Failure Risk**: Critical alerts may not be delivered
  - Mitigation: Multiple notification channels and delivery confirmation
